# The six tools, and what each one sets.
#
# **Every press says `Tools:` first.** The rail offers all six, and Tool options offers the four
# selection tools again whenever one of them is up -- deliberately, so changing between them does
# not mean crossing the window and coming back. That puts Lasso, Rect, Wand and Move on screen twice
# the moment the first one is pressed, and the harness refuses a name that fits two controls. Which
# is the point of it: the two are numbered differently, and an unqualified `press Rect` resolved to
# whichever came first.
#
# The rail is the subject here. `contextual.txt` is where the panel's copies are driven, and
# `select.txt` alternates between the two on purpose.
expect tool Brush
expect select-tool -
press Tools:Eraser
expect tool Eraser
press Tools:Lasso
expect select-tool lasso
press Tools:Rect
expect select-tool rect
press Tools:Wand
expect select-tool wand
press Tools:Move
expect select-tool move
press Tools:Brush
expect tool Brush
expect select-tool -
shot tools-brush
